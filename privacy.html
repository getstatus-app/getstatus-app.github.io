THE SHORT VERSION

Everything you create or capture in the App stays on your device. Status media, recovered messages, saved files and private copies of media are processed entirely on your phone and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We run no servers and hold no data of that kind about you, so there is no account, no profile, and none of your content for us to hand over or lose.

The App is free and is funded by advertising served by Google AdMob: a single banner at the bottom of the screen, and rewarded video ads that play only when you choose to watch one. To show these ads, the App includes Google's advertising SDK and uses the Internet permission, and the ad SDK collects a device advertising identifier and related technical data as described under ADVERTISING below. That is the only thing that leaves your device - none of your statuses, saved files, recovered messages or any other content you create in the App is ever transmitted. An optional paid subscription removes the ads; payment for it is handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see your payment details (see PURCHASES AND SUBSCRIPTIONS below).

PERMISSIONS THE APP USES AND WHY

The App requests only the permissions needed for its features:

Internet: used only by the Google AdMob banner to fetch ads and report ad measurement. The App never uses it to upload, back up, or transmit any of your statuses, saved files, or captured messages - those stay on the device.

Photos, videos and storage access: used to read status media that other apps store on your device, to save copies you choose into your gallery, and - if you turn on the recovery features below - to read the messaging app's received photos and videos so the App can keep on-device copies. On Android 13 and above the App requests Android's "Photos and videos" permission for this; on older versions it uses the storage-read permission. Separately, because the WhatsApp status folder is hidden from normal media access on Android 11 and above, the App asks you once to pick that folder in Android's own folder picker so it can show your statuses, after which it can read only the folder you picked. The App does not request or hold the "All files access" permission, and you can review or revoke any of this at any time in system settings or by clearing the App's data. All of this happens entirely on your device.

Notification access (optional, off until you grant it): used only for the "Recover deleted messages" feature. If you grant this access in system settings, the App reads incoming WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business message notifications (the sender or chat name, the message text, and the time) and stores them privately on your device, so a message stays readable even if the sender later deletes it. Notifications from every other app are ignored. Nothing read from notifications ever leaves your device. You can revoke this access at any time in system settings, and turning the feature off inside the App also deletes everything it has captured.

Notification posting: used only to tell you when the App has caught media that was deleted, so you can open it before it is pruned. You can turn these off in your system notification settings.

Keep device awake: after a message arrives, the App may keep the device awake for a few seconds so it can finish making its private on-device copy of newly received media before the device goes back to sleep. This is used only by the "Recover deleted messages" feature.

App visibility: used only to detect whether the relevant messaging app is installed, and to open or share media with apps you choose. Nothing about which apps you have installed is recorded or transmitted.

All file reading, viewing, capturing, and saving happens locally on your device. No file, message, filename, or any other content of yours is ever uploaded or transmitted.


DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

App settings (such as language and theme preferences) are stored locally on your device and are removed if you uninstall the App.

Saved statuses are copies of media you explicitly choose to save. They are placed in your device's gallery and remain under your control; you can delete them at any time using your gallery or file manager.

Recovered messages and deleted media (optional feature): while "Recover deleted messages" is switched on, the App keeps captured message text and copies of recently received photos and videos so they remain available if the sender deletes them. About these copies:

 - the App never uploads any of them anywhere;
 - the captured message text is kept in the App's internal storage, which no other app can read;
 - working copies of media are kept in the App's own folder under Android/data (on Android 11 and above this is inaccessible to other apps; on Android 8 to 10, an app you have granted storage permission could in principle read it), are deleted automatically after 7 days, are capped at 500 MB with the oldest removed first, and are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so the App never copies them to your Google Drive;
 - when a photo or video is confirmed to have been deleted for you, the App also saves that one copy into a "GetStatus/Recovered" folder inside your device's Pictures, so it is not lost if you clear the App's data or reinstall the App. Because this folder is part of your shared storage, it appears in your gallery, other apps that have media access can read it, and it may be included in any gallery or photo backup you have turned on yourself (for example Google Photos). These are ordinary files you own: you can delete them at any time from the App, your gallery, or a file manager;
 - turning the feature off, or using the clear button inside the App, deletes the captured text and the App's working copies immediately;
 - uninstalling the App removes the message log and the App's private copies. Copies already saved into your Pictures/GetStatus and Pictures/GetStatus/Recovered folders stay in your gallery, like any other photo, until you delete them.

Older statuses (optional feature): if you enable "Keep older statuses", the App keeps private copies of statuses you have seen in its app-private storage for up to 7 days, so you can still view them after they expire. These copies follow the same rules as above.


ADVERTISING

The App shows a single banner advertisement at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). This is how the App stays free. In addition, the App's settings offer a rewarded video advertisement, also served by AdMob: it plays only when you tap the option, never on its own, and watching it to the end turns ads off for 1 day.

To serve and measure these ads, the AdMob SDK collects and shares with Google a device advertising identifier (the resettable Advertising ID) together with technical data such as your device type, general (coarse) location inferred from your IP address, and ad-interaction events (an ad was shown, tapped, etc.). This is standard for apps that show AdMob ads. Google acts as an independent controller of that data; its use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and its Advertising policies.

What the ad SDK does NOT receive: none of your statuses, saved files, recovered messages, captured notification text, or private media copies. Those never leave your device and are never shared with Google or anyone else.

Your choices:
 - Reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings (typically Settings > Privacy > Ads). Deleting it stops apps, including this one, from receiving a stable advertising identifier.
 - Opt out of personalised ads in the same settings screen; you will still see ads, but not personalised ones.

We do not control what data Google collects through the ad SDK or how Google uses it. While the optional remove-ads subscription is active, the App loads no ads at all. If advertising is ever removed from the App entirely, this policy will be updated and the Internet and advertising-identifier permissions will be dropped again.


PURCHASES AND SUBSCRIPTIONS

The App offers one optional purchase: a yearly "remove ads" subscription, bought through Google Play's billing system.

All payment is handled by Google Play (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). We never see, receive or store your card, UPI or bank details - Google does not give them to us. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

The only record the App keeps is a yes/no note on your device of whether the subscription is currently active, which it refreshes by asking Google Play. We run no servers, so we hold no purchase history about you. The subscription is tied to your Google account, so it is restored automatically if you reinstall the App, and you can manage or cancel it at any time in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.


OTHER PEOPLE'S MESSAGES AND MEDIA

The recovery feature necessarily holds content created by other people - the text of messages they sent you and their names as shown in the notification. We never receive any of it, and it is never used for any purpose beyond showing it back to you.

Because that content belongs to those people rather than to you, please treat it accordingly: use it only in ways the sender and the law allow, and do not republish or forward it without permission. Depending on where you live, retaining or sharing another person's messages may carry legal obligations of your own.

Before the feature can be switched on, the App shows you a screen explaining exactly what it will read, what it will store, and how long it keeps it. Notification access stays inactive until you grant it in system settings.


DATA SHARING

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we never share any of your content - statuses, saved files, or messages - with anyone. There is nothing to sell or share: no data of yours ever reaches us or any third party.

Sharing a saved status or recovered media with another app happens only when you explicitly tap Share, and it uses Android's standard share system under your control.


YOUR RIGHTS AND HOW TO EXERCISE THEM

Because we operate no servers and hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export, or delete - and no account to close. That also means we cannot identify you or link anything to you.

To remove data held on your device:

 - Turn off "Recover deleted messages" - this immediately deletes everything it has captured. The clear button inside the App does the same without turning the feature off.
 - Turn off "Keep older statuses" - this immediately deletes the cached expired statuses.
 - Delete saved copies from your gallery or file manager.
 - Uninstalling the App removes all of the above, plus your settings.

SECURITY

We hold no central store of your data, so there is no server-side database to breach - which removes the most common source of privacy incidents entirely.

On your device, the App relies on the Android application sandbox and your device lock screen. The captured message log is kept in the App's internal storage, which other apps cannot read. Please note that it is stored as ordinary text and is not separately encrypted by the App: anyone who can unlock your phone, or who has physical access to an unlocked device, can read it through the App. If that matters to you, keep the recovery feature switched off.

Automatic cloud backup is disabled for this App, so neither the message log nor the private media copies are uploaded to your Google account.

If we become aware of a vulnerability that affects your data, we will fix it and describe the fix in the App's release notes. Where a law requires us to notify users or a regulator of a personal-data breach, we will do so within the period that law prescribes.
